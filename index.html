A LETTER FROM MY HEART
To MISTY, the sweetest unexpected gift in my life… 💌

I don’t know if words can ever explain how much you mean to me, but I want to try. Since you became part of my life, my days have felt softer and the little things have meant so much more. Your presence is never ordinary to me. It is a gift I treasure, a comfort I return to, and a laugh I hope will always find its way into my days.

I want you to know that you never have to be strong every single day or hide your sadness behind a smile. You can come to me tired, quiet, confused, or hurting. You never have to explain your feelings to deserve care. I will always try to be a safe place for you—a place where you can speak freely, be yourself, and be reminded of your worth whenever you forget it.

MISTY, I wish you could see yourself through my eyes, even for one moment. You would see how beautiful your soul is, how gently your heart deserves to be treated, and how your presence leaves a mark no one else could. I wish you dreams that come true, lighter days, and people who give you the same kindness you so freely share. Thank you for being exactly who you are, with all the little details that make you irreplaceable.

And if you ever forget how much you matter, come back to this page. Let it remind you that someone sincerely wishes you well, celebrates your happiness, and hopes life will be as gentle with you as you deserve. I am grateful to have you in my life, and I hope our friendship keeps growing through honesty, laughter, and beautiful memories. ♡

Always cheering for you, MISTY ♡
